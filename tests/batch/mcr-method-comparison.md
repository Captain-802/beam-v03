# Eigen vs standard M<sub>cr</sub>: outlier analysis (19 Sep 2026 campaign)

## Definitions

- **Ratio** = M<sub>cr,eigen</sub> / M<sub>cr,standard</sub> exactly as the engine prints it (`ltb.McrRatio`) and the
  batch flags it (cross-check iv, band 0.85 to 1.25). *Same segment* means the closed form is evaluated for the
  segment and the combination of the eigen design value (the governing span of a multi-span member); *across the two
  runs* compares the eigen design value with the standard run's own design value (the whole member, one segment
  L<sub>E</sub> = k L).
- **Direction of conservatism.** The task text describes "ratio > 1" as the closed form giving a higher M<sub>cr</sub>;
  with the engine's definition it is the opposite: ratio > 1 means the closed form gives the **lower** M<sub>cr</sub>
  (conservative); **ratio < 1 means the closed form gives the higher M<sub>cr</sub>, i.e. the standard method is
  unconservative there**. Every statement below uses the engine's ratio and says explicitly which side is unconservative.
- The batch (`tests/batch/results.md`, 170 cases / 296 runs) flags 11 same-segment outliers and 51 across-run outliers.
  All are explained below; two findings led to engine changes (UB-27, UB-40) and one recommendation is left open
  (overhangs on the closed-form route).

## 3. Findings and recommendations

- **F1 (fixed, engine defect, unconservative):** quarter-point / mid-span sampling of the closed-form C<sub>1</sub> at a moment jump (applied in-span couple) took the far-side ordinate. `mAtStation()` (eurocode-checks.js) now takes the larger side; affected only diagrams with an in-span couple at L/4, L/2 or 3L/4 of the segment (UB-27, MIX-01 in the library). Recorded in AUDIT.md; regression in `tests/campaign.test.cjs`.
- **F2 (fixed, eigen route, contradictory input silently accepted):** the destabilising switch with every z<sub>g</sub> = 0 produced an eigen PASS with the load at the shear centre (UB-40, 0.84) while the same beam with the top-flange height entered fails (UB-52, 1.06). The eigen route now blocks PASS for that input state; the closed-form route keeps its ×1.2 L<sub>E</sub> treatment.
- **F3 (open, recommendation R1):** the SN003a closed form is evaluated with fork ends over the whole member for overhang layouts (a free end at x = L). In the five library cases it was conservative by 26 to 153 %, but the assumption is not the code's; the standard route should either block overhangs (as it blocks unpublished C<sub>2</sub> cases) or evaluate the back span as the segment between its supports and hand the overhang to SN006a-type treatment. Not changed in this campaign (verdict-affecting design decision; the eigen route covers the layout).
- **F4 (open, information):** UB-44 prints a closed-form M<sub>cr</sub> 47 % above the eigenvalue when C<sub>2</sub> is not published; the verdict is blocked, but the printed number should be read with the block.
- The Table B.3 mid-span moment M<sub>s</sub> used for C<sub>m</sub> (`cmTableB3`, `a.Mh`) still samples a single side of a jump at mid-span; it only matters for N<sub>Ed</sub> ≠ 0 with an applied couple exactly at mid-span (no library case). Left as is, noted in AUDIT.md.
