The September 2026 structural audit, corrections, reference basis and remaining limitations are documented in [AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md).

Run the regression suite with `node --test tests/*.test.cjs` (Node 22+; 86 tests). After editing source, run `./build-single-html.ps1` in PowerShell to regenerate `dist/beam-design-single.html`, then rerun the tests. The single-file distribution must match the modular source.

**Elastic critical moment M<sub>cr</sub> (EC3, not fully restrained):** the `M<sub>cr</sub> method` select under *Axial & lateral-torsional buckling* sets `S.mcrMethod` to `eigen` (default) or `standard`. `eigen` solves M<sub>cr</sub> by the finite-element eigenvalue method in `js/08-mcr-eigen-patch.js` for the actual moment diagram, per-load heights z<sub>g</sub>, lateral restraints, support conditions and internal hinges (span by span for continuous beams). `standard` delegates to the closed-form implementation `checksEC3UnrestrainedSCI()` in `js/checks/eurocode-checks.js` (kept alive by the patch as `window.checksEC3UnrestrainedStandard`): C<sub>1</sub> from the NCCI SN003a Table 3.2 rows recognised from the load list of the governing combination (simply supported or fixed-ended full-span UDL / central point load: 1.127/0.454, 1.348/0.630, 2.578/1.554, 1.683/1.645), the SCI end-moment curve when only couples act ("Not Loaded", self-weight allowed) or the diagram is linear, or the Serna quarter-point expression for a general diagram (C<sub>2</sub> unpublished); M<sub>cr</sub> = C<sub>1</sub>(&pi;&sup2;EI<sub>z</sub>/L<sub>E</sub>&sup2;){&radic;[I<sub>w</sub>/I<sub>z</sub> + L<sub>E</sub>&sup2;GI<sub>t</sub>/(&pi;&sup2;EI<sub>z</sub>) + (C<sub>2</sub>z<sub>g</sub>)&sup2;] &minus; C<sub>2</sub>z<sub>g</sub>} with G = 81000 N/mm&sup2;, NCCI SN006a for I/H cantilevers, the same chain with I<sub>w</sub> = 0 for hollow sections (&chi;<sub>LT</sub> curve from one shared `ltbCurveNA()`, NA Table NA.1, on both routes) and the P385/P362 channel &kappa; chain, with L<sub>E</sub> = L<sub>E</sub>-factor &times; L over the whole member - the route MasterSeries-type software prints; the eigensolver is not run. **Design basis** of the standard route is the M<sub>cr</sub> chain (`ltb.MbRd = ltb.MbMcr` = &chi;<sub>LT,mod</sub>W<sub>y</sub>f<sub>y</sub>/&gamma;<sub>M1</sub> &le; M<sub>c,Rd</sub>, also handed to the Eq 6.61/6.62 interaction); the P362 Expn 6.55 simplified slenderness (`ltb.MbSimp`) is printed for comparison only. **Load height** on the standard route is one z<sub>g</sub> per combination, `stdZgFor()`: the most destabilising signed per-load value among the loads active in the combination (an upward load at +z<sub>g</sub> is stabilising, so its sign is reversed; default `S.za` when no per-load value exists); it is always printed (`ltb.zgNote`), applied only where SN003a publishes C<sub>2</sub>, and a destabilising z<sub>g</sub> on any other diagram pushes a blocking message (`ltb.zgBlocked`) unless the destabilising &times;1.2 L<sub>E</sub> switch carries it (advisory; a further advisory when both treatments overlap). Both routes expose `ltb.McrStandard`, `ltb.McrEigen` (null in standard mode) and the MasterSeries-style C<sub>1</sub> inputs `ltb.c1in = {M1, M2, Mo, psi, mu}` (segment end moments, M<sub>2</sub> the larger; mid-segment moment above the chord; &psi; = M<sub>1</sub>/M<sub>2</sub>; &mu; = M<sub>o</sub>/M<sub>2</sub> capped at 300); in eigen mode the comparison (`mcrStandardFor(a, sec, seg, {fb, factors})`) describes the LTB-governing combination's own diagram, loads and z<sub>g</sub>, and the report names the method in its heading, prints the `C1 = fn(M1, M2, Mo, psi, mu)` line in standard mode and the ratio M<sub>cr,eigen</sub>/M<sub>cr,standard</sub> in eigen mode.

**MasterSeries-format design brief (EC3):** for `S.code === 'EC3'` the report opens with a "Design Brief" rendered by `renderMasterSeriesBrief(a, c, sec)` in `js/06-brief-masterseries.js` &mdash; a pure function of the analysis result, the check result and the section (no DOM) that prints the checks in the order, wording and three-column layout of a MasterSeries "MasterSteel Beam Design to EN 1993-1-1" printout (label | formula with substituted values | result + tag), as specified line by line in [docs/BRIEF_MAPPING.md](docs/BRIEF_MAPPING.md). Variants: Beam &amp; Beam-Portion (no axial force, M<sub>z</sub> = 0) and Axial with Moments; fully restrained (`M<sub>b.Rd</sub> = M<sub>c.y.Rd</sub>  Fully Restrained`), closed section ("not susceptible to LTB"), cantilever (SN006a chain in standard mode), channel (&kappa; chain), and both `S.mcrMethod` routes (FE eigenvalue lines, or `C<sub>1</sub> = fn(M<sub>1</sub>, M<sub>2</sub>, M<sub>o</sub>, &psi;, &mu;)` / `M<sub>cr</sub> = Fn(C<sub>1</sub>, L<sub>e</sub>, I<sub>z</sub>, I<sub>t</sub>, I<sub>w</sub>, E)` in standard mode). Every number printed is a field of the check object (V<sub>pl.z.Rd</sub>, A<sub>v,z</sub> and M<sub>c.z.Rd</sub> come from the engine's `c.ax` block) or is derived by one of the small pure `msb*` helpers (N<sub>Rk</sub>, M<sub>y.Rk</sub>, k<sub>c</sub>, &psi;, &mu;, unity-bar maximum) with the formula shown; no resistance is recomputed. Blocking messages (`c.unsupported`) appear as red `NOT VERIFIED` rows in the block of the check they concern (a message no block claims is printed before the deflection block) and again in the footer, the title carries `(FAIL)` or `(NOT VERIFIED)`, ratios above 1 carry a red `Warning` tag, a failing brief has the light-blue MasterSeries panel, and the unity bar ends with `Max` (deflection excluded). The existing detailed report follows under a collapsible "Detailed derivation" heading. `tests/brief.test.cjs` renders the demo beam, a UB with axial compression and M<sub>z</sub>, a PFC with an eccentric load, a cantilever, an SHS, a two-span member and a Class 4 case in both M<sub>cr</sub> methods and checks the block order, the unity-bar `Max`, the printed M<sub>c.y.Rd</sub> and M<sub>y.Ed</sub>/M<sub>b.Rd</sub> values against `c.McRd` / `c.ltbUtil`, and that every blocking message is printed.

**Automatic pattern loading, uplift, cantilever strut length and per-segment deflection limits (19 Sep 2026 gap closure, group G1; AUDIT.md "19 Sep 2026 gap closure"):**

- *Pattern loading* (`S.autoPattern`, default on): for a member with more than one span segment (`spanSegments()`: support to support plus any cantilever/overhang; internal hinges do not split segments) every enabled ULS and SLS combination with a non-zero Q factor is followed by the generated combinations "Q on span *i* only", "Q on spans *i*+*i*+1 only" and "Q on odd/even spans only" (`expandPatternCombos()`; duplicates dropped), G/W/E at their entered factors on every span (EN 1990 6.10 as entered). `comboLoadPieces(combo)` is the one pattern-aware load list every consumer reads (bending, torsion FE, P385 list, standard-route C<sub>1</sub>/z<sub>g</sub>/SN006a shape recognition, C<sub>m</sub>, eigen LTB per combination); a distributed load straddling a boundary is split there, and while patterns are active distributed Q loads are split at the interior boundaries for every combination so the solver x-grid stays identical. With the switch off nothing is generated or split and the results equal the previous ones exactly. Limitation (printed): &gamma;<sub>G,inf</sub> = 1.0 on relieving spans is not generated.
- *Uplift / hold-down*: every ULS and SLS combination's reactions are tested; a support lifting in a ULS combination prints "Hold-down required: R = &minus;x kN at support n (combination &hellip;)" and blocks PASS unless the support's "hold-down provided" box is ticked (advisory with the design force); a support lifting only in SLS combinations (the Q-only deflection case has no G) is an advisory that names the force and the un-generated EQU set-A combination. Member Forces block of the brief; reactions line of the report; BS 5950 path included.
- *Cantilever strut*: single fixed support with N<sub>Ed</sub> &gt; 0 takes L<sub>cr</sub> = 2.0 L about both axes while the L<sub>E</sub>/L factor is 1.0; any other factor is kept as an override; the basis is printed.
- *Deflection*: each segment against its own limit &mdash; span/`S.divisor` between supports, L/`S.divisorCant` (default 180, UK NA Table NA.2 cantilever row [verify]) for a cantilever segment, both capped by the optional absolute limit `S.deflAbs` (mm); a cantilever value is the tip deflection relative to its support. The brief and the report print the limit used per segment.

Regression tests: `tests/gap-closure.test.cjs` (hand-computed continuous-beam table values, statics, pre-change parity figures). Run the batch campaign with `node tests/batch/run-batch.cjs` (see tests/batch/README.md).

**Web transverse forces, EN 1993-1-5 clause 6 with the 7.2 interaction (19 Sep 2026 gap closure, group G2; AUDIT.md "19 Sep 2026 gap closure, group G2"):** on the EC3 path (fully restrained and both unrestrained routes) `webTransverseCheck(a, sec, fy, eps, cl)` in `js/checks/eurocode-checks.js` checks every point load and every support reaction as a patch load on the web: load types (a) interior, (b) through the web (a point load directly over a support, F<sub>Ed</sub> = max(P, R)) and (c) near an unstiffened end (evaluated together with (a) whenever s<sub>s</sub> + c &lt; 2h<sub>w</sub>/3, the lower F<sub>Rd</sub> governing); k<sub>F</sub> per Figure 6.1, F<sub>cr</sub> = 0.9k<sub>F</sub>Et<sub>w</sub>&sup3;/h<sub>w</sub>, m<sub>1</sub>, m<sub>2</sub> (one re-evaluation with m<sub>2</sub> = 0 when &lambda;&#772;<sub>F</sub> &le; 0.5), l<sub>y</sub> per 6.5(3)/(4), &chi;<sub>F</sub> = 0.5/&lambda;&#772;<sub>F</sub> &le; 1, F<sub>Rd</sub> = f<sub>yw</sub>L<sub>eff</sub>t<sub>w</sub>/&gamma;<sub>M1</sub>; &eta;<sub>2</sub> + 0.8&eta;<sub>1</sub> &le; 1.4 with &eta;<sub>1</sub> = M<sub>Ed</sub>/M<sub>c,Rd</sub> (+ N<sub>Ed</sub>/N<sub>pl,Rd</sub>) at the same station of the same ULS combination. Inputs: per point load `ld.ss` (stiff bearing length, mm, blank = 0) and `ld.stiff`; per support `sp.ss` (blank = the section flange width B as a typical seating, tagged [verify]) and `sp.stiff` ("bearing stiffener provided": the station prints "stiffener declared - design stiffener separately (EN 1993-1-5 9.4)" as an advisory instead of the check and bounds the panel length a of the other stations; without any declared stiffener a = L, printed). Sections: rolled I/H, PFC (one-sided flange), RHS/SHS (two webs of thickness t with the tabulated flat depth, flange share B/2 per web, load shared by the lever rule of its eccentricity). Verdict entries `Web transverse force  F_Ed/F_Rd (EN 1993-1-5 6.2)` and `Web transverse force + bending (EN 1993-1-5 7.2)` (worst station each; a failing station is a FAIL with a "Bearing stiffener required" advisory). Brief: block "Web Transverse Forces (EN 1993-1-5 cl 6)" directly after Local Capacity (geometry, m<sub>1</sub>/m<sub>2</sub>, a, the governing station's full chain, one table row per station, unity cells `F/F_Rd` and `Web 7.2`); report: block after Moment Resistance with the same content. Regression tests `tests/web-transverse.test.cjs` (hand-derived values, see AUDIT.md); batch cross-check (viii-FRd). Not evaluated (printed): distributed loads as patch loads, hanger loads, the closely spaced total-load check (6.3(3)), flange-induced buckling (section 8), the stiffener itself (9.4); the BS 5950 path keeps its cl 4.5 advisory.

**Classification, effective area, M-V-N, k<sub>c</sub> floor, RHS k<sub>ij</sub>, channel torsional buckling, high-shear M<sub>v,Rd</sub> for every family, restraint forces (19 Sep 2026 gap closure, group G3; AUDIT.md "19 Sep 2026 gap closure, group G3"):**

- *Minor-axis classification of I/H* (`classifyEC3`, `mzFlangeStress`): an applied M<sub>z</sub> does not stress the web of an I/H section, so the web keeps its y-y bending (+N) limits and the flanges are classified as outstands under the combined stress (the outstand compressed by M<sub>z</sub> is wholly in compression, &alpha; = 1, so the 9&epsilon;/10&epsilon;/14&epsilon; bound governs; the stresses are printed). Channels and hollow sections keep the conservative uniform-compression bound. A UB with M<sub>z</sub> is now evaluated (Class 1/2 when its geometry is) instead of NOT VERIFIED.
- *A<sub>eff</sub> of a Class-4 web in uniform compression* (`aeffWebCompression`, EN 1993-1-5 4.4, &psi; = 1, k<sub>&sigma;</sub> = 4, &rho; = (&lambda;&#772;<sub>p</sub> &minus; 0.22)/&lambda;&#772;<sub>p</sub>&sup2;, b<sub>eff</sub> split 0.5/0.5): used in N<sub>c,Rd</sub>, in N<sub>b,Rd</sub> (&lambda;&#772; = &radic;(A<sub>eff</sub>f<sub>y</sub>/N<sub>cr</sub>)) and in Eq 6.61/6.62 with the Table 6.7 Class-4 column (N<sub>Rk</sub> = A<sub>eff</sub>f<sub>y</sub>, W<sub>eff,y</sub> = W<sub>el,y</sub> when the flanges stay Class &le; 3, assumption printed). The former "web Class 4 in uniform compression" block is gone; a 1016&times;305&times;249 with N = 3000 kN evaluates with A<sub>eff</sub> = 0.905A. Class 4 under the combined stress gradient and Class-4 flanges still block.
- *cl 6.2.10* (`shearReducedResistances` + the station sweep, `c.mvn`): at every ULS station with V<sub>Ed</sub> &gt; 0.5V<sub>pl(,T),Rd</sub> and N<sub>Ed</sub> &ne; 0 or M<sub>z</sub> &ne; 0 the shear area takes (1 &minus; &rho;)f<sub>y</sub>: N<sub>V,Rd</sub>, M<sub>v,y,Rd</sub>, M<sub>v,z,Rd</sub>, a<sub>V</sub>, then the 6.2.9.1 chain (M<sub>N,V,Rd</sub> with the (4) waiver, Eq 6.39/6.40 for RHS, linear for Class 3 and channels); uniaxial = plain ratio, biaxial with the &alpha;/&beta; exponents. Verdict entry "Bending+shear+axial (6.2.10)", brief row after the coexistent M-V row, unity cell "M-V-N".
- *High-shear M<sub>v,Rd</sub> for every family*: I/H Class 3 (W<sub>el,y</sub> &minus; &rho;I<sub>web</sub>/(h/2))f<sub>y</sub> [conservative], channels (W<sub>y</sub> &minus; &rho;t<sub>w</sub>h<sub>w</sub>&sup2;/4)f<sub>y</sub>, RHS/SHS (W<sub>y</sub> &minus; &rho;t(h &minus; 2t)&sup2;/2)f<sub>y</sub>; the I/H Class 1/2 form is unchanged. Applied at the peak-moment station (`c.McRd`, `c.mvForm`) and in the span-wise sweep (`c.coex`); the family/class blocks are gone.
- *k<sub>c</sub> floor* (`kcFromC1`): k<sub>c</sub> = 1/&radic;C<sub>1</sub> &ge; 1/&radic;2.76 = 0.60 (Table 6.6 lower bound) on both M<sub>cr</sub> routes; printed when it bites (`ltb.kcFloored`).
- *Table B.1 RHS row*: RHS/SHS Class 1/2 k<sub>zz</sub> = C<sub>mz</sub>(1 + (&lambda;&#772;<sub>z</sub> &minus; 0.2)n<sub>z</sub>) &le; C<sub>mz</sub>(1 + 0.8n<sub>z</sub>), k<sub>yz</sub> = k<sub>zz</sub> (`buck.rhsRow`).
- *PFC torsional / torsional-flexural buckling* (`torsionalFlexuralBuckling`, cl 6.3.1.4): y<sub>0</sub> = e<sub>sc</sub> from `js/sections/pfc-section-data.js` (SCI P385 Table A.3), i<sub>0</sub>&sup2; = i<sub>y</sub>&sup2; + i<sub>z</sub>&sup2; + y<sub>0</sub>&sup2;, N<sub>cr,T</sub> = (GI<sub>T</sub> + &pi;&sup2;EI<sub>w</sub>/L<sub>T</sub>&sup2;)/i<sub>0</sub>&sup2; with L<sub>T</sub> = L<sub>cr,z</sub> or the new input `S.LT`, N<sub>cr,TF</sub> from the monosymmetric cubic coupled with the y-y (axis of symmetry) flexural mode, &chi;<sub>T</sub> on curve c (Table 6.2, U-sections [verify]), N<sub>b,T,Rd</sub> = &chi;<sub>T</sub>Af<sub>y</sub>/&gamma;<sub>M1</sub> in the verdict and the lower &chi; in the 6.61/6.62 axial terms. The 6.3.1.4 block is gone.
- *Restraint design forces* (`restraintForces`, advisory): 2.5 % of N<sub>f,Ed</sub> = M<sub>Ed</sub>/h at every intermediate lateral restraint and every support torsional restraint (EN 1993-1-1 5.3.3 / 6.3.5.2 basis), in the brief's Lateral Restraint Portions table and a report block.
